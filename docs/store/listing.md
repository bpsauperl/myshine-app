## English

- **Subtitle** (30): Your Shine aligner treatment
- **Promotional text** (170): Which aligner today, when to change, your plan in 3D and messages with your clinic. For patients of Shine clinics.
- **Keywords** (100): aligners,clear aligners,Shine,orthodontics,braces,teeth,dentist,clinic,treatment,schedule
- **Description:**

  myShine is your companion during Shine clear aligner treatment. Your clinic gives you access with a one-time code.

  TODAY: see which aligner you wear and when you change to the next one.

  CHECK IN: when your doctor uses check-ins, confirm each change, say how the new aligner fits and add photos if you like.

  YOUR PLAN: see your treatment plan in 3D once your doctor shares it.

  SCHEDULE: every change date, from the first aligner to the last.

  MESSAGES: ask your clinic a question or send a photo.

  DOCUMENTS: sign your treatment papers and keep them in one place.

  REMINDERS: a reminder on the day you change.

  myShine does not diagnose and never changes your treatment. Your doctor decides every step. Your clinic is responsible for your data; there are no ads and no tracking.

  You need a code from your Shine clinic to sign in.
